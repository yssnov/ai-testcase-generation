## Main pain named by the respondent (Q1, Q5)

- Most time-consuming part of the work: preparing test data, environment and access after deployment to the test stand; writing automated tests.
- Most tedious / annoying part, in their words: repeated work when the implementation differs from the requirements and autotests written from the specification have to be adapted or rewritten almost from scratch.
- Already tried to simplify it themselves? Yes: templates for test documentation, reusable checklists, written descriptions of repeated workflows; AI (Claude skill) to generate autotests.
- Is this pain related to test design from requirements? **partly** (tests prepared from the specification become invalid when requirements and implementation diverge).
